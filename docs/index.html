    How the score works. Each stock is compared against the others on 3‑month and 1‑month price momentum, distance from its 50‑day average, and the tone of its recent headlines, with a penalty for stretched RSI readings. 50 is average for this list. A high score means recent strength and upbeat coverage; it does not predict future returns. This is a research aid, not financial advice.
