Market headlines

    How the buy and sell signals work. Buy zone: the stock is in an uptrend (price and 50‑day average above the 200‑day average) and is pulling back to its 50‑day average, dipping to oversold, or showing strong momentum without being overbought. Take profits: RSI is 75+ or price is 15%+ above its 50‑day average. Sell / avoid: price is below both averages in a downtrend, or the stock is weak and under its 50‑day average. Everything else is Hold / wait. The stop‑loss idea sits two average daily ranges (ATR) below the price. These are mechanical chart rules that react to the past; they don't predict the future.
    How the score works. Each stock is compared against the others on 3‑month and 1‑month price momentum, distance from its 50‑day average, and the tone of its recent headlines, with a penalty for stretched RSI readings. 50 is average for this list. This is a research aid, not financial advice. Check fundamentals, valuation and your own risk tolerance before buying anything.
